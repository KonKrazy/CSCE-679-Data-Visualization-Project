# Tasks

Use the dashboard to answer each question. Say your answer out loud when you are done.
"Behind" means below the national baseline (z < 0). The tool starts on
Cambodia · Household wealth · 2010; it is reset before each task.

## Spotting the Gap

**T1a.** In **Cambodia in 2014**, which region is furthest behind the national baseline
on **household wealth**?

**T1b.** In **Indonesia in 2017**, how many regions are more than 1 standard deviation
behind (z < −1) on **literacy**? Name them.

## Connecting the Dots

**T2a.** Take the region from T1a (Cambodia, 2014). Apart from household wealth, on which
indicator is it furthest behind?

**T2b.** In **Peru in 2012**, consider the regions that are behind on **household
electricity**. How many of them are also behind on **literacy**?

## Watching the Journey

**T3a.** For the region from T1a, is its household-wealth gap **closing or widening**
between 2000 and 2014?

**T3b.** In **Cambodia**, which region improved its household-wealth position (z) the
most between 2000 and 2014?
